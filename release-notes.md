## UI improvements
- Home ends at the two buttons. The Recent list is gone from that screen, so nothing is cut off at the bottom.
- The Report last hit and File a damage report buttons now sit side by side on phones like the S22 Ultra too.

## Bug fixes
- Live data on Home works again: turning Power on starts logging right away, and it keeps going until you turn Power off or stay stopped for the auto-off time (30 minutes by default). A short stop no longer ends it.
- The Stop button in the notification now also turns Power off, so the app and the notification agree.

## Other
- New setting "Turn off when not driving" (30 minutes by default). Power turns itself off after the phone has been stopped that long, with a notification to turn it back on.
- Car Bluetooth is now an optional prompt (off by default). Pick your car's device and BumpLog offers to start logging when it connects and to stop when it disconnects.
- Passenger mode now goes back to Driver by itself after 3 hours, but never while you are moving. You get a heads-up with an Extend button, then an Undo button, and the status line shows the time left.
- Updates keep your data.
